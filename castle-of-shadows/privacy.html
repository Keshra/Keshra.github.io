Castle of Shadows: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Castle of Shadows ("the app", package com.kez.castleofshadows) handles, why, and your choices. The app is developed by an independent developer ("we").

Summary

  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email or contacts. Signing in to Google Play Games for the Speed Run world leaderboard is optional.
  • Your game saves, best times and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads (see below).
  • The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Data stored on your device

The knight name you type, saved games, your best Speed Run time, your graphics-quality, sound and button-layout settings, your map view settings and whether you own "Remove Ads" are stored locally in the app. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have bought "Remove Ads", the app shows a full-screen ad at natural breaks in play, at most once every three minutes. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate location),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped),
  • crash logs, diagnostics and other app performance data.

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games and the leaderboard

The Speed Run mode can post your escape time to a world leaderboard provided by Google Play Games. This is optional: you are asked to sign in only when you open World ranking in Settings. If you sign in, Google shares your Play Games player name, profile picture and player ID with the app, and the app sends your Speed Run time to the leaderboard. Your name and time are then visible to other players on that leaderboard. We do not receive your Google account email address. You can sign out or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app; see Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

Feedback form

Settings > About > Send feedback / report a bug opens a Google Form in your browser. What you type there, plus the game name and app version, which the form is pre-filled with, is sent to us through Google Forms. Do not enter anything you do not want us to see; if you give an email address we use it only to reply to you. The form is optional.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment information under its own privacy policy. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase was made, so that it can turn ads off and restore the purchase when you reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general/teen audience.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell any personal data. The only data we receive is what you choose to type into the feedback form. Data collected by Google for ads, purchases or Play Games is shared with and retained by Google as described in its policies.

Your rights

You can withdraw ad consent in the game (Privacy button), reset your advertising ID in Android settings, sign out of Play Games, or stop all processing by uninstalling the app. To ask us to delete a feedback message you sent, email us at the address below. For requests about data held by Google, use Google's data & privacy tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
